SVCf2 recipient selection and registration

234c4 does not directly call the SYS dispatcher. It reads a recipient ID
from byte[8428+selector]; selector1 is used by the RLC packet builders.
Zero ID returns2f. Recipient record is [6054]+92*ID. Byte+34 selects one
of four available slots; no slot returns31. Additional caller-state and
packet/stack address conditions can return2e/32 and have not been shown
satisfied at runtime.

After local conditions, code prepares a saved frame: packet pointer at+8,
mode at+c, and recipient entry from record+4 at savedPC+3c. It also updates
slot and caller bookkeeping and calls2457c. Thus the missing delivery edge
is dynamic recipient registration plus frame handling, not a hidden direct
BL to the SYS routine.
