RLC alternate save and direct-call root coverage

Separate root9ad0..9b6b now decoded:57 listing instructions bytechecked,
56 reached by localCFG. It saves architectural registers into context and
uses ROTW to walk register windows, then sets a1=0 before CALL0 9b70 at9b2a.
Thus the shared save routine records context+cc=0 on this path, which causes
restore9e61/9e64 to skip its stack-copy block, provided this saved field is
preserved. The alternate entry does not provide an additional stack-copy
invocation under that condition. Its window-register semantics remain manual.

No direct call to9ad0 in the current listings, and no full little-endian
5fff9ad0 literal anywhere in pinned decompressed firmware. Computed/vector
entry is not ruled out. Absence of a literal is not proof of unreachability.
