Offline RLC fixed-store and indexed-interface follow-up

Scope: manual instruction inspection of the existing pinned IRAM listings.
Addresses retain the inferred IRAM/DRAM/aperture mapping; no hardware execution.

04664 and 046c4 are fixed-port reads into RAM, not parameterized MMIO writers.
They select zero at 70000000, initialize/poll ports 60037254 / 600372a4,
then copy paired reads from 60037258 / 600372a8 into the adjacent ranges
5ffee464..5ffee4ec and 5ffee4ec..5ffee574 (exclusive ends). Each is 136 bytes.
The source header identifies these ports as RLC_SPM_ACCUM_DATARAM_ADDR/DATA
and RLC_SPM_ACCUM_SWA_DATARAM_ADDR/DATA (segment 1 offsets 3c95/96/3ca9/aa).

01b0c writes its argument to fixed 60058a00 and polls fixed 60058a60.
01bec selects 59 at 70000000 and writes/polls fixed 60010030; its final
store at 1c74 is s8i into RAM 5ffee260, confirmed by alternate-target decode.
Neither routine supplies a caller-selected MMIO destination.

05138 and 0542c cache 64 results from 05348 for indices 0,2,...,126 in
RAM 5ffed9e4..5ffedae4 (exclusive end). 05210 restores those same indices
through 053b8 using cached words and then calls 05138.

053b8 does accept an index and value, but its fixed interface is RTAVFS:
  a2 -> 6003ac00 (GRTAVFS_RTAVFS_REG_ADDR)
  a3 -> 6003ac04 (GRTAVFS_RTAVFS_WR_DATA)
  1  -> 6003ac10 (GRTAVFS_RTAVFS_REG_CTRL)
  poll bit0 of 6003ac14 (GRTAVFS_RTAVFS_REG_STATUS), clear CTRL, poll clear.
05348 uses CTRL=2, polls bit1, reads 6003ac0c, clears CTRL, polls clear.
The source gc_10_3_0_offset.h names segment-1 offsets 4b00/01/03/04/05
and independently gives block base 3ac00. Both helpers select zero at 70000000.
The helper itself has no explicit index range check; that does not establish
that its RTAVFS index namespace reaches GC CC registers. No CC route proven.

These findings explain selected unresolved scanner destinations: constants
lost across special-register synchronization and calls are not evidence of
caller-selected addresses. They do not close all unresolved RLC stores.
